# Third-party software and assets

This repository intentionally does **not** vendor the historical Reuleaux source tree, OpenRAVE, MoveIt, ROS, institute robot CAD/mesh assets, standards PDFs, or downloaded research papers.

The project workflow depended on:

- ROS / RViz
- Reuleaux
- OpenRAVE / IKFast
- MoveIt IKFast utilities

Those projects remain subject to their own licenses and distribution terms.

The original thesis working directory also contained institute/project robot models and literature used for research. Their presence in the historical workspace is not evidence that they may be republished. For that reason, this portfolio repository contains only the author's documentation, parameterization, helper scripts, and non-restricted result descriptions unless redistribution rights are separately confirmed.
